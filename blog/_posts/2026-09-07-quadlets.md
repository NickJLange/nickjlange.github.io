I've been hankering to move to quadlets in the hope of simplifying my admin burden. After a wasted Sunday evening, I think the verdict is:

- I like, this is a win for simplicity.
- Why no one has put forth ```userctl``` as an alias for ```systemctl --user``` is beyond me.

[ManPage](https://docs.podman.io/en/latest/markdown/podman-systemd.unit.5.html)
[ArchLiux](https://man.archlinux.org/man/quadlet.5.en)

It's far easier to template quadlet generation versus podman-compose. It's easier to survive reboots (the catalyst for the conversion was an upgrade to Ubuntu LTS 26). 

### What was non-trivial

1. It took a long while for me to get my head around environmental variables, how they fit, and the relationship to instance variables. 
1. I lost some time on container structure cleanup (podman network access is still a PITA)
1. ```podlet``` is really not worth your time. I wasted more time conforming to the conversion tool than just learning the syntax. Sorry podlet.


### What remains

1. How do I store these (or some protofile) in source control
1. One weird issue with an environmental variable not being passed on container startup

In the end, I think for my goal of having six or seven agents all using the same base configuration with instance config - I have a winner. Longer term, all that's left is to find a remote command and control mechanism for viewing logs and I'm starting and stopping these Systm D units. And I'll be in heaven. Until then, I'll be farming this out to my agents.
